## Current location

The opening scene is complete as a strong working draft. It follows an ordinary child walking beside a parent along a river, discovering a stack of stones, and choosing to add one stone to something another conscious being began.

## Last completed work

The scene established its intended emotional rhythm:

- quiet observation;
- curiosity before explanation;
- suspense as the child raises and places the stone;
- release when balance returns and the world resumes its movement;
- contribution expressed through a small action rather than explanation.

The final image is the child leaving one more stone behind before returning to the waiting parent.
